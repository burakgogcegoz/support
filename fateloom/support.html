Frequently asked

What is energy?

Energy powers each choice in a life. On the free tier it refills over time; you can also watch a rewarded ad for more, or top up threads to keep playing.

What are threads?

Threads are Fateloom's in-game currency. They keep a life going once your energy runs out, and can unlock premium archetypes.

What does Premium include?

Premium unlocks the best AI storyteller, illustrated scene images, and a larger energy cap.

I bought something but didn't receive it.

Purchases are restored automatically on the next launch. If an item is still missing, email us with your account email and the approximate purchase time and we'll sort it out.

How do I delete my account?

Email privacy@fateloom.co from your account email and we'll delete your account and data.

Set up support@fateloom.co and privacy@fateloom.co (or substitute your own addresses) before submitting to the stores.
