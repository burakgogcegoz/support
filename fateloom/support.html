Frequently asked

What is energy?

Energy powers each choice in a life. It refills over time; you can also watch a short optional ad for more, or use threads to keep playing.

What are threads?

Threads are Fateloom's in-game currency. They keep a life going once your energy runs out, and pay for rich narration and new scene images.

What does Premium include?

Fateloom Premium is a monthly auto-renewing subscription: illustrated scene images and 3× daily energy. Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions; you keep Premium until the end of the paid period.

Do I keep worlds and archetypes I buy?

Yes — they are one-time purchases, yours forever. On a new device, sign in with the same account and tap Wallet → Restore purchases.

I bought something but didn't receive it.

Open the Wallet and tap Restore purchases. If the item is still missing, email us your account email and the approximate purchase time.

The story said something inappropriate or wrong.

Tap ⚑ Report under that passage. Reports are reviewed and used to improve our filters. The story is AI-generated fiction.

How do I delete my account?

In the App: Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, lives, cards and notebook. Active subscriptions must be cancelled separately in your store account. Without the App: see Delete your account.
