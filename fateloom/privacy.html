This Privacy Policy explains how Fateloom ("we", "us") handles information when you use the Fateloom mobile app (the "App").

Information we collect

  • Account: your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Gameplay: the lives you create and the choices you make, so we can save and continue your game.
  • Purchases: a record that a purchase occurred and what it granted. Payments are processed by Apple or Google — we never receive your card details.
  • Voice input: if you dictate an action, the audio is sent for transcription and is not retained after transcription.
  • Advertising identifiers: our ad partner may process a device identifier to deliver rewarded ads.
  • Basic technical data: app logs needed to operate and secure the service.

How we use information

  • To provide and save your game, and to power the AI narration.
  • To process purchases and grant in-app items and energy.
  • To show rewarded ads on the free tier and credit the reward.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

Third-party services

  • OpenAI — generates the story text from a compact summary of your current life; it does not receive your account credentials.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — process payments and validate purchases.
  • ironSource (Unity) — serves rewarded video ads on the free tier.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your account and game data until you ask us to delete it. To request deletion of your account and associated data, email privacy@fateloom.co from your account email.

Children

Fateloom is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

  • You can limit ad tracking in your device settings.
  • You can request access to, or deletion of, your data at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@fateloom.co.
