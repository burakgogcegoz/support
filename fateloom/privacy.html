Privacy Policy

Effective: 2 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fateloom ("we", "us"), developed by Burak Göğcegöz, handles information when you use the Fateloom mobile app for iOS and Android (the "App"). Contact: burakgogcegoz@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account: your email address and a securely hashed password; or, if you sign in with Apple or Google, the account identifier and email address that provider shares with us (Apple's "Hide My Email" relay addresses work too). Guest play creates an anonymous account with no email. We never receive your Apple or Google password.
  • Gameplay: the lives you create, the choices and free-text actions you type or speak, the story the App generates, and your cards, titles, goals, daily quests and culture notebook — so we can save and continue your game.
  • Purchases: a record of what you bought and what it granted (store transaction id, product, subscription expiry). Payments are processed by Apple or Google — we never receive your card details.
  • Voice input (optional): if you dictate an action, the recording is sent for transcription and deleted afterwards; we keep only the resulting text as part of your game.
  • Advertising data (optional rewarded ads): our ad partner may process your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS only if you allow tracking; AAID on Android), IP address and basic device information to serve and measure ads. We pass only an internal user id so the reward can be credited.
  • Reports: if you report a story passage, we store the passage and the reason you chose.
  • Technical data: server logs (time, request, error codes) and usage/cost records of AI requests, needed to operate, secure and budget the service.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or health data.

How we use information

  • To provide, save and continue your game and to generate story text, scene images and narration.
  • To process and verify purchases with Apple or Google and grant threads, worlds, archetypes and Premium.
  • To show optional rewarded ads and credit their rewards.
  • To review reported content, prevent abuse and keep the service secure.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for profiling beyond what is described here.

Sharing

We share data only with the service providers below, solely to run the App. If you tap Share, we create a public link containing only that summary (character name, story highlights, cards); anyone with the link can view it.

Service providers

  • OpenAI — generates story text, scene images and voice transcription from a compact summary of your current life and your action. It does not receive your account credentials. Under OpenAI's API terms, data sent through the API is not used to train their models.
  • Apple and Google — sign-in (if you choose it), payments and purchase validation.
  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and its ad networks — serve optional rewarded video ads.
  • Microsoft Azure — hosts our servers and database (EU, West Europe region).

Tracking (iOS)

On iOS the App asks for permission before any cross-app tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are still shown but without your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in device settings.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your account and game data until you delete your account. You can delete it at any time inside the App: Settings → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent and removes your account, lives, cards, notebook, reports and AI-usage records. You can also request deletion without the App — see Delete your account. Server logs are kept for up to 30 days; store purchase records may be kept where the law requires it.

Children

Fateloom is intended for users aged 13 and older (or the minimum age in your country) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will delete the account.

Your rights

  • You can access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to or restrict processing, by contacting us.
  • If you are in the EU/UK, our legal bases are performance of a contract (providing the game), consent (tracking, voice input) and legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention). You may complain to your local data protection authority.
  • California residents: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising without your permission.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored on access-restricted servers; passwords are hashed and API keys are encrypted at rest.

Changes

We will post updates here and change the effective date. Significant changes will be announced in the App.
