Live a life. See where fate takes you.

Fateloom is an AI-narrated life simulation. You're born into a world, you make the choices — but the outcome is never fully yours to command. Every playthrough is a wholly new life.

A whole life, your way

An AI storyteller narrates your life from first cry to last word, adapting to every choice — pick one, type your own or just say it.

Fate has plans too

Funny, dramatic or life-changing events crash in. People remember what you did. Set a life goal and see if you get there.

Build a dynasty

When a life ends, continue as your child — inheritance, siblings, old family feuds, three generations and beyond.

Worlds & legends

Be born in today's cities or travel back to Istanbul 1910, Edo Japan, Renaissance Florence — or live as a legend and change history.

Learn as you live

Pick up local words, sayings and customs along the way; your culture notebook keeps them, with little quizzes.

Collect your legacy

100+ cards to flip open, titles that give your next life a head start, daily quests and shareable obituaries.
