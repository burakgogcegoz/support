Delete your Fateloom account

Fateloom: Life Stories · developer: Burak Göğcegöz

Option 1 — in the App (instant)

  1. Open Fateloom and sign in.
  2. Tap Settings on the home screen.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2 — by email (no App needed)

Email burakgogcegoz@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Fateloom account" from the email address of your account (or, for Apple "Hide My Email" accounts, the relay address shown in your Apple ID settings). We confirm and delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, sign-in identifiers, password hash)
  • All lives, story text, choices and generated images
  • Cards, titles, goals, quests, culture notebook and settings
  • Thread balance, unlocks, purchase and reward records, content reports and AI-usage records

What is kept

  • Server logs for up to 30 days (security), then deleted.
  • Apple and Google keep their own purchase records under their policies. An active Premium subscription is not cancelled by deleting your account — cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.
