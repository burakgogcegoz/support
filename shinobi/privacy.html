Privacy Policy

Shinobi: Elemental Defense · Last updated: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shinobi: Elemental Defense (the “Game”), published by CerebroCraft (“we”, “us”), handles information. The Game does not require you to create an account. We do not ourselves collect personal information from you; however, the Game shows advertising through a third-party service, and that service collects limited device data as described below.

Information We Collect

We (CerebroCraft) do not ask you to create an account and do not request your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location, and we do not operate servers that store personal data about you. The only data collection that occurs is performed by our advertising provider (see “Advertising” below) to show and measure ads.

Game Data Stored on Your Device

Your progress — such as level completion, unlocked upgrades, and in-game currency — is saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Deleting the Game removes this data.

Advertising

The Game displays advertisements, including optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch, through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), an ad-mediation service, and its partner ad networks. To serve and measure these ads, these partners may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) and other device identifiers;
  • technical information such as device model, operating system, language, and IP address; and
  • ad-interaction data, such as which ads were shown, clicked, or completed.

This information is used to deliver ads, measure their performance, limit repetition, and prevent fraud, and — depending on your settings — to show more relevant (personalized) ads. For details, see the ironSource / Unity advertising privacy policy at https://www.is.com/privacy-policy/.

Your Choices

You control ad tracking through your device settings. On iOS, you can decline tracking when prompted (App Tracking Transparency) or change it under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization under Settings › Privacy › Ads. Declining tracking does not remove ads, but it limits the use of your advertising identifier for personalization.

Children’s Privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the Game contains third-party advertising, we encourage parents and guardians to supervise children’s use of the Game and to apply the device-level ad and tracking controls described above.

Data Security

We do not operate servers that store your personal data. Data handled by our advertising provider is protected under that provider’s own security practices and privacy policy. Locally saved game data is protected by your device’s own security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
