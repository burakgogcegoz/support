Privacy Policy

Shinobi: Elemental Defense · Last updated: August 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shinobi: Elemental Defense (the “Game”), published by CerebroCraft (“we”, “us”), handles information. We designed the Game to respect your privacy: it works fully offline and does not require an account.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The Game does not ask you to create an account, does not request your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or any other personal data, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Game Data Stored on Your Device

Your progress — such as level completion, unlocked upgrades, and in-game currency — is saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Deleting the Game removes this data.

Third-Party Services

The current version of the Game does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. If we add such services in a future update (for example, optional advertisements), we will update this policy and, where required, request your consent within the Game before any data is shared.

Children’s Privacy

The Game is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any information from children. Because we collect no personal data from anyone, no personal data is collected from children.

Data Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data for us to secure on a server. Locally saved game data is protected by your device’s own security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
