Privacy Policy

Testimony: Live Logic — last updated 19 August 2026

Testimony does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and the app makes no network requests at all.

What is collected

Nothing. This is not a summary of a longer answer — the app has no server, no analytics library, no crash reporter, no advertising, and no code that sends anything anywhere. It runs entirely on your device and works in aeroplane mode.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally so it is still there next time you open it:

  • your current level and the recent-results history the difficulty uses
  • your best score and best streak, and how many rounds you have played
  • your settings: language, theme, pace, sound and haptics
  • which one-off explanations you have already been shown

This stays on your device. It is never transmitted, and nobody but you can read it. Deleting the app deletes it permanently; there is no backup and no way for us to restore it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data whatsoever, it collects no data from children either. There is no chat, no user-generated content, no social feature and no link out of the app.

Third parties

There are none. No advertising network, no analytics provider, no cloud service, no third-party SDK that transmits data. Apple and Google may collect their own information about app downloads under their own policies, which we neither control nor receive.

Your rights

Regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data held about you. We hold none, so there is nothing to access or delete on our side. The data on your device is under your control and is removed completely when you delete the app.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app collects anything, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change. The store listing's privacy declarations will be updated at the same time.
