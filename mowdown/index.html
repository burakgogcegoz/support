About the game

You are a spinning blade loose on the lawn. Cut grass to grow, and the bigger you get the bigger the things you can take — hedges, benches, fences, then whole trees, and eventually the rival blades circling the same map. Seven maps, a campaign that ramps from a gentle park to a last-blade-standing derby, contracts to chase mid-round, and a garage of blade designs and finishes to unlock.

Frequently asked

Does the game need an internet connection? The game itself plays offline. Ads and leaderboards need a connection when they are used.

Do I need an account? No account is required.

How is my progress saved? Locally on your device. Deleting the game deletes it. See our Privacy Policy.

I bought the premium unlock and lost it. Use Restore Purchases on the store screen. If that does not bring it back, email us with your receipt and we will sort it out.

Can I turn off ads? The premium unlock removes the between-round ads. Ads you choose to watch for a reward stay available.

Privacy

We have no servers and no accounts, and your progress never leaves your device. The game does show ads, and those services collect some device information. Read the full Privacy Policy.
